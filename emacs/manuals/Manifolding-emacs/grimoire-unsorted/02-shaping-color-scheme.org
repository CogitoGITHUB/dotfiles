* Shaping Color Scheme
A versatile color scheme system with two main temporal variants (Day & Night) and three style flavors (Formal, Smooth, Cyberpunk). Each variant can be rendered with either intense contrast or soft contrast depending on the user's preference.

** Variants
- Day
- Night

** Style Options
- Formal :: clear, sharp, high readability, classic contrast
- Smooth :: gentle, low contrast, easy on the eyes
- Cyberpunk :: extreme contrast, neon-like intensity, digital aesthetics

** Day Variant
*** Formal
    - Intense: White background, black text (#FFFFFF / #000000)
    - Soft: Light grey background, dark grey text (#E6E6E6 / #333333)
*** Smooth
    - Intense: Off-white background, dark grey text (#F9F9F9 / #1A1A1A)
    - Soft: Light grey background, medium grey text (#EEEEEE / #555555)
*** Cyberpunk
    - Intense: Bright white background, neon cyan/magenta text (#FFFFFF / #00FFFF or #FF00FF)
    - Soft: Dark grey background, neon blue/pink text (#222222 / #00CCFF or #FF33AA)

** Night Variant
*** Formal
    - Intense: Black background, white text (#000000 / #FFFFFF)
    - Soft: Dark grey background, light grey text (#1A1A1A / #CCCCCC)
*** Smooth
    - Intense: Very dark navy background, soft white text (#0A0A1A / #EDEDED)
    - Soft: Dark charcoal background, light grey text (#2B2B2B / #DDDDDD)
*** Cyberpunk
    - Intense: Black background, neon green/pink text (#000000 / #39FF14 or #FF0080)
    - Soft: Dark purple background, neon cyan/magenta text (#1A001A / #00FFFF or #FF33AA)

** Notes
- "Intense" emphasizes sharp contrast for focus and visibility.
- "Soft" reduces eye strain with gentler tones while maintaining readability.
- Cyberpunk uses high-saturation accent colors on dark backgrounds to create a futuristic, digital aesthetic.
- Each main variant (Day/Night) is optimized for temporal lighting conditions.
