* Shapeshifter’s Music from Code

Prologue :manifesto: Live coding is music written in lightning—structures summoned while the audience watches the storm.
Instruments are galaxies of muscle memory; code is the telescope and the gravity lens.
This scroll collects the ideas behind the practice: philosophy, pedagogy, dramaturgy, ethics, and aesthetic law.
No snippets, no syntax—just the choreography of thought.

The Shape of Sound-Thought :theory: ** Music as Language of Transformations


Melody: a path through a space of possible intervals.

Harmony: agreement on which doors are “legal” to open together.

Rhythm: negotiated time—contracts signed in groove.

Timbre: geometry of vibration—texture chosen like brush grain.

Form: the city plan where motifs commute.


** Live Coding’s Thesis

Visibility: composition is performed, not hidden.

Reversibility: ideas can be rewound, refactored, reframed mid-flight.

Composability: tiny mental units stack into architectures.

Parametricity: feeling becomes a dial you can actually turn.

Narrative: the audience witnesses decisions, not just results.


** Axioms for Shapeshifting

1. Every parameter is a poem if you listen at the right scale.


2. Grooves are social contracts; break them with consent.


3. Beauty emerges when control meets surprise in respectful combat.


4. Time is clay. You’re allowed finger marks.



The Shapeshifter Method :method: ** The Three Looms


/Pattern Loom/: where repetition learns to breathe.

/Harmony Loom/: where vertical choices ripple horizontally.

/Texture Loom/: where timbre sculpts emotion without permission.


** The Four Rituals

1. Summon: establish a living pulse the audience can trust.


2. Bend: deform predictability until curiosity wakes.


3. Reveal: show your hand—name the change as you make it.


4. Resolve: land the craft cleanly or refuse landing on purpose.



** The Tensegrity Model of a Set

/Struts/ (fixed anchors): motif, tempo home, tonal home.

/Cables/ (tensioned freedoms): density, syncopation, register.

Stability emerges from tension, not from cement.


Theory Without Notation :learning: ** Modes as Moods (Narrative Index)


Ionian :: sunlight at noon; everything onscreen is high contrast.

Dorian :: leather jacket in the rain; serious but generous.

Phrygian :: streets after midnight; narrow alleys of semitone.

Lydian :: horizons tilt upward; gravity feels dialed down.

Mixolydian :: bonfire chorus; cadences keep a wink in reserve.

Aeolian :: steady wind inland; dust, memory, footsteps.

Locrian :: scaffolding without walls; deliciously unsafe.


** Harmony as Gravity Wells

Consonance attracts; dissonance orbits; modulation slingshots.


** Rhythm as Civics

Meter is governance; syncopation is civil disobedience.

Polyrhythm is federalism: multiple valid clocks negotiating resources.


Pedagogy Blueprint :teaching: ** Learning Loop (for self or workshop)


1. Set a pulse you can hum walking.


2. Name a constraint (only upward intervals, only odd lengths).


3. Vary one dimension (density, accent map, register).


4. Speak your intention aloud; then act.


5. Audit the change: keep, revert, or fork.


6. Archive the story, not the settings.



** Concept Drills (Story-Only)

Ghost Accent Walk: march down a corridor; every third tile glows; sometimes you step heavy, sometimes you pass like fog.

Mirror & Shadow: a melody stares into a mirror; the reflection moves a heartbeat later and a step lower.

Tilted Horizon: keep the same skyline but rotate the camera two degrees every minute; harmony “brightens.”


Performance Design :stagecraft: ** Audience Contract


Promise clarity of intent early.

Promise novelty later.

Promise a landing—or declare in advance that you’re chasing comets.


** Dramaturgy of a 30-Minute Set

1. /Handshake/ (3–5 min): establish pulse + tone palette; let them breathe with you.


2. /First Portal/ (4–6): reveal the first structural trick (odd grouping, modal tint).


3. /Expansion/ (8–10): interleave textures; ratchet density carefully.


4. /Fracture/ (5): break the contract; introduce risk visibly.


5. /Reweave/ (4–6): integrate the fracture into a larger pattern; leave a gift on the doorstep.



** Visual Rhetoric Without Screenshots

Movements should be legible. Change one family at a time.

Name changes verbally like chapter titles; narrate your thinking briefly.

Use silence as punctuation, not apology.


Aesthetics :taste: ** The Crisp & The Grain


Crisp: strong edges, minimal smear, pointillist rhythm.

Grain: smeared harmonics, foggy edges, velvet noise. Balance prevents sugar or sand from choking the ear.


** Constraint as Style

Pick three “verbs” for the set: weave, erode, ignite.

Veto list: three things you refuse to do tonight; this sharpens silhouette.


Ethics of the Algorithm :ethics:


Credit Lineage: ideas have ancestors; introduce them.

Explain the Trick: don’t mystify to gatekeep—mystify to enchant, then unpack.

Consent to Surprise: destabilize with care; audiences are bodies, not graphs.

Accessible Design: tempo, loudness, and brightness belong to everyone in the room.


Practice Without Presets :practice: ** Rituals for Daily Craft


10-minute pulse meditation: walk, clap, breathe—the metronome inside you.

One-knob day: choose a single dimension to study (swing %, release shape, interval size) and journal the emotional curve.

Listening sprints: catalog micro-textures you hear on the street; name them poetically.


** Archiving the Invisible

Save decisions as stories: “I brightened the skyline until the birds started following.”

Track why a choice worked, not only that it worked.


Collaboration & Ensemble :together: ** Conversation, Not Collision


Leave sonic space like you leave air in a sentence.

Ask questions musically; answer in the other’s dialect before returning to yours.


** Roles Without Hierarchy

Cartographer: maps the current terrain.

Navigator: proposes the next waypoint.

Caretaker: maintains headroom and emotional safety.


Show Types :formats:


Clinic: slow, explanatory, annotated movement.

Ritual: minimal speech; changes labeled by lighting or gesture.

Cinema Ear: preplanned narrative beats with improvised connective tissue.

Assembly: audience contributes constraints by vote or cards.


The Library of Constraints :palette: A living index of idea-seeds to spark sets (purely descriptive).


Odd symmetry corridors (walk 5, breathe 3, return on 2).

Tilted cadences (resolve upward, but refuse to settle).

Crescendo by subtraction (make it feel bigger by removing anchors).

Color swap (keep the rhythm skeleton; repaint the timbre skin).

Echo as ethics (repeat the audience’s chant back transformed, not louder).


Story Scores (No Code, Only Tale) :stories: ** The Clockmaker’s Garden A gardener keeps twelve fountains timed to different moons.
At dusk, one fountain stutters; the others lean in to help, trading drops until a new river path emerges.
By midnight, the stutter is the heartbeat, and the garden dances around it.


** The Bridge of Feathers Travelers cross a canyon on a bridge that grows with each step.
Some planks appear early, some late; wind patterns decide the sequence.
They reach the far side together because each footfall teaches the bridge how to be a bridge.

** The Window That Refused Winter In a gray city, one window chooses to stay warm-colored all season.
Passersby begin walking in the rhythm of that glow.
Eventually the whole block syncs to a brightness that was never forced—only offered.

Curriculum Map :roadmap: ** Phase I — Pulse & Pattern Literacy


Embodied time practice, narrative journaling of groove, constraint design. ** Phase II — Harmony as Topology

Modal mood catalog, “gravitational” storytelling of cadences, modulation arcs. ** Phase III — Texture & Space

Spectral metaphors, depth staging, psychoacoustic empathy. ** Phase IV — Dramaturgy & Audience

Set architecture, risk choreography, accessibility playbook. ** Phase V — Research & Publication

Document the lineage of your ideas; publish annotated performances with stories.


Failure Modes & Anti-Patterns :antipatterns:


Endless parameter fiddling without narrative consequence.

Surprise used as jump scare, not as earned revelation.

Complexity cosplay: dense for density’s sake.

Ignoring the room: brilliance that forgets to be kind.


Metrics That Don’t Kill the Magic :metrics:


Did the audience breathe with you? (watch shoulders)

Could a first-timer retell your set in three beats?

Did you learn a new rule you didn’t know you believed?


Closing Vow :epilogue: This isn’t a toolkit; it’s a covenant with time.
We will make choices in public, and we will be gentle with our mistakes.
We will treat parameters as feelings with names.
We will leave people brighter than we found them.

Appendices :appendix: ** Glossary of Poetic Terms (Non-Technical)


Ghost Accent: an emphasis the ear expects but the body withholds.

Gravity Tone: the pitch where the room keeps returning even when you don’t.

Horizon Tilt: gradual brightening or darkening of harmony without changing the skyline.

Density Weather: how crowded the air feels, independent of loudness.

Legibility: the audience’s ability to perceive the intention behind a change.

Which fracture taught me the most?

Where did silence do more work than sound?

What will I refuse next time to sharpen my silhouette?
